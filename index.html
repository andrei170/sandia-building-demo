The questions homeowners ask.

Will my details be shared with other contractors?

No. When you contact us directly the enquiry stays with our office. You will not get calls from other firms because you filled in our form.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family replacements are a day or two depending on size, pitch and how much decking needs replacing once the old roof is off.

Repair or replace — how do I know?

Age, the condition underneath, and whether the problem is one area or the whole roof. We will tell you honestly if a repair buys you several more years.

Do you handle insurance claims?

Yes. We document the damage to an insurer's standard and meet the adjuster on site. We will also tell you when there is not enough damage to be worth claiming.

Do you do commercial as well as homes?

Both. Residential and commercial roofing and property restoration across Central New Mexico.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, and we will show you the documentation before any work starts.
