Why calling a contractor directly gets you a better job.

What happens on a lead website

  • Your details are sold to several contractors at once
  • Three or four firms call you within the hour
  • Each one is racing the others, so the visit is rushed
  • The winner is whoever quotes lowest, not whoever is right

What happens when you call us

  • One company has your details and nobody else does
  • We come when it suits you, not in a race
  • We can spend the time to look properly
  • You get one price we can stand behind for years
A cheap price from a contractor who was rushing to beat three others is not a saving. It is a roof you will be dealing with again.
